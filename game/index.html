The game is running slowly because your browser is not using your graphics card.
  1. In Chrome, open Settings, then System.
  2. Turn on "Use graphics acceleration when available".
  3. Click Relaunch, then open the game again.

Using Edge? The same switch is in Settings, then System and performance.

Who needs help

Your shift at the help desk

You are the office IT technician. People will ask you for help. Fix their problems to earn XP and get promoted.

  1. Watch the list on the right. When someone needs help, their name appears there and a ! floats above them.
  2. Walk over to them. Use WASD or the arrow keys. Hold Shift to walk faster.
  3. Press E to help. Read their problem and pick the fix a good technician would try first.
Wrong answer? You get a hint and one more try, worth half the XP.
Stuck? Get a hint before answering. It costs 25% of the XP.
Be quick. Each request has a timer. Urgent ones are worth double.
Some fixes need you there. Follow the blue marker to the printer or parts cabinet, then go back to the person.
Camera: drag with the mouse to turn, scroll to zoom.
